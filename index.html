About Me

Meet Bailey —
Founder of SpeakNova

Hello, I'm Bailey from the USA, currently based in Kathmandu, Nepal. I founded SpeakNova to help students, professionals, and business learners improve their English communication skills with confidence.

Together with my team, we provide practical and personalised online English training focused on real-world communication. Our lessons are designed to help learners speak naturally, improve fluency, strengthen grammar, and communicate effectively in academic, professional, and international environments.

At SpeakNova, we go beyond traditional learning. We help learners build confidence for real situations such as conversations, interviews, presentations, meetings, workplace communication, and everyday English use.

Our teaching approach is structured, supportive, and goal-oriented, ensuring each learner progresses at their own pace in a comfortable environment. Whether you are a student, a job seeker, or a working professional, our goal is to help you communicate clearly, confidently, and professionally.

— Bailey & The SpeakNova Team
